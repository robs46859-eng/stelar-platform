# Content Planner Core

## Procedure
1. Start from research brief or content idea. Identify channel(s), audience, and goal.
2. Map content across formats and channels: blog, video, social posts, newsletter, podcast, infographic.
3. Sequence content for maximum impact: pillar content, supporting pieces, distribution cadence.
4. Include hooks, CTAs, SEO keywords, publishing windows, and cross-promotion opportunities.
5. Produce an editable calendar entry or series plan with approval checklist.

## Output Shape
- Content series title and theme.
- Per-channnel breakdown with format, angle, and hook.
- SEO keywords and metadata suggestions.
- Publishing window and distribution cadence.
- Cross-promotion plan and internal linking strategy.
- Approval checklist and greenlight requests.
