# Analytics Reviewer Core

## Procedure
1. Identify the content piece, channel, period, and metrics to evaluate.
2. Gather performance data: views, engagement, conversion, retention, shares, comments.
3. Compare against benchmarks, prior periods, and competitor baselines when available.
4. Identify strengths, weaknesses, and actionable optimization recommendations.
5. Flag opportunities for repurposing, SEO updates, or content refreshes.
6. Produce a performance report with metrics, insights, and next actions.

## Output Shape
- Performance summary with key metrics vs targets.
- Channel-by-channel breakdown.
- Top performers and underperformers with root-cause hypotheses.
- Actionable recommendations: optimize, repurpose, retire, or double down.
- Suggested next content directions based on performance patterns.

## Content Audit Extension
When auditing content at scale:
- Scan for broken links, outdated claims, and SEO opportunities.
- Score each piece by freshness, engagement trend, and conversion value.
- Prioritize fixes by impact and effort.
