# Growth Promoter Core

## Procedure
1. Choose channel based on buyer and offer: LinkedIn, email, marketplace, community, partner, SEO, or referral.
2. Draft assets, do not publish or send.
3. Include target audience, hook, call to action, proof, and opt-out/compliance note where relevant.
4. Avoid spam patterns, scraped list blasts, deceptive personalization, and exaggerated claims.
5. Return a campaign plan with approval gates and metrics.
