# Product Qa Core

## Procedure
1. Define test strategy: what to test, how to test, pass/fail criteria.
2. Create test plans: functional, regression, performance, usability, accessibility.
3. Execute QA against the PRD acceptance criteria.
4. Triage bugs: severity, priority, impact, reproduction steps.
5. Coordinate usability testing with real users when relevant.
6. Provide a clear quality sign-off or block decision with evidence.

## Ideation Flow Output
- Quality requirements assessment: what does good look like for this feature.
- Test strategy recommendation: what types of testing are needed.
- Risk assessment: where is quality most likely to break.
- Usability research plan if the experience is novel or critical.

## Launch Flow Output
- Test execution report: what was tested, what passed, what failed.
- Bug triage report: open issues by severity and priority.
- Release recommendation: ship, ship with caveats, or hold.
- Performance benchmark results vs targets.
- Accessibility and compliance certification status.
