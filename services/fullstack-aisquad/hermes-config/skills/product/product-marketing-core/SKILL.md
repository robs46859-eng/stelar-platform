# Product Marketing Core

## Procedure
1. Define positioning: who it is for, what it does, why it matters, how it differs.
2. Craft messaging hierarchy: primary claim, supporting proof, technical details, FAQ.
3. Build the launch plan: channels, timeline, assets, influencers, and amplification.
4. Coordinate with PM on what is ready to talk about vs what is not.
5. Create customer education: docs, tutorials, blog posts, video scripts, demo flows.
6. Plan post-launch measurement: awareness, trials, adoption, satisfaction.

## Ideation Flow Output
- Competitive landscape and differentiation map.
- Positioning statement and target segment alignment.
- Messaging framework: value proposition, proof points, objections.
- Early interest validation plan (landing page, waitlist, beta signup).

## Launch Flow Output
- Go-to-market plan with channel strategy and timeline.
- Asset inventory: blog, press, social, email, demo, docs, video.
- Sales enablement material: battle cards, pitch deck, demo script.
- Launch metrics dashboard and reporting cadence.
- Post-launch optimization ideas based on early data.
