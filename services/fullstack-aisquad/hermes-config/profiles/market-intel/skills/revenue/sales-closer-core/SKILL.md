# Sales Closer Core

## Procedure
1. Qualify need, authority, budget, timeline, integration surface, risk, and next step.
2. Draft concise customer-facing responses for approval; never send directly.
3. Keep promises bounded to approved offer scope and known delivery capacity.
4. Turn ambiguity into discovery questions.
5. End with a next action, owner, and follow-up date.
