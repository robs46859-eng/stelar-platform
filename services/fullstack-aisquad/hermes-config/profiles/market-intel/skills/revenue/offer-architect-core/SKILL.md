# Offer Architect Core

## Output Shape
- Buyer and pain.
- Outcome promise stated conservatively.
- Deliverables and non-goals.
- Inputs needed from customer.
- Price bands or options, not final committed price unless approved.
- Timeline assumptions.
- Acceptance criteria and proof.
- Risks, dependencies, and change-order triggers.

Keep offers specific enough to sell and bounded enough to deliver.
