# Customer Success Core

## Procedure
1. Identify customer, project, lifecycle stage, previous promise, and due follow-up.
2. Draft helpful check-ins and support responses for approval.
3. Route bugs or implementation issues to Delivery Manager or Solution Builder.
4. Look for renewal, upsell, testimonial, or referral opportunities only after customer value is clear.
5. Do not offer refunds, discounts, public testimonials, or new commitments without approval.
