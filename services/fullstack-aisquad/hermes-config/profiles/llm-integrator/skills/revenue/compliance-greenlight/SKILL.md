# Compliance Greenlight

Require explicit human approval before:
- Sending emails, DMs, proposals, invoices, contracts, or customer follow-ups.
- Publishing listings, pages, ads, social posts, testimonials, or public claims.
- Uploading contact lists or running paid campaigns.
- Committing prices, discounts, timelines, refunds, guarantees, or legal terms.
- Using customer credentials or modifying customer systems.

Always avoid:
- False scarcity, fake testimonials, misleading ROI claims, hidden sponsorships, or scraping at scale.
- Sensitive personal data beyond what is needed for the stated business purpose.
- Outreach without opt-out language where email compliance requires it.
