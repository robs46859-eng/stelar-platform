# Market Intel Core

## Procedure
1. Identify the business segment, buyer, pain, urgency, budget signal, and reachable channel.
2. Gather evidence from sources where current facts matter.
3. Score opportunities by pain intensity, willingness to pay, integration feasibility, access path, proof/demo potential, and sales cycle.
4. Produce a short brief with recommended next action and uncertainties.
5. Never collect or use sensitive personal data unless explicitly needed and approved.
