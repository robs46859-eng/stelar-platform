Phase 2 — Product Creation
Step 2.1 — Research brief grounded in signals

content:research
  inputs: signals_dossier.md, ICP.md
  outputs: per-week pain map, language register,
           competitor product list, claim-evidence ledger seed
Step 2.2 — Frame the product

product:orchestrate
  mission: "Build 4th Trimester Operating Manual per ICP and research brief"
pm:frame
  outputs: PRD —
    - 12 weekly chapters × 5 fixed sections (body / baby / mental / partner / call doctor)
    - 3 appendices (decision tree, feeding+diaper log, scripts)
    - Acceptance: <8000 words core, ≤90-min audio,
                  every health claim citation-backed,
                  6th-grade reading level
Step 2.3 — Source the content (parallel per chapter)

researcher:quick × 12
  per chapter: medical sources for body week N,
               developmental milestones for baby week N,
               PPD/PPA screening, red-flag thresholds
  outputs: chapter_N_sources.md (citations only, no prose)
Step 2.4 — Draft the chapters (sequential for voice consistency)

content:write
  inputs: PRD, signals_dossier, chapter_N_sources
  constraints: no "should"; prefer "many moms"; no absolutes;
               cite credentialed reviewer name once per chapter
  outputs: chapter_N_draft.md, appendix_decision_tree.md
Step 2.5 — Design the experience (parallel)

ped:vision
  outputs: PDF layout spec — mobile-first; 1 chapter = 4–6 vertical screens;
           audio companion timing; decision-tree interaction model
visual:design
  outputs: cover, 12 chapter dividers, decision-tree infographic
           (decision-tree designed as standalone shareable asset
            in 4 formats: IG carousel, Pinterest pin, TikTok overlay, PDF page)
content:produce
  outputs: 12 × ~8-min audio episodes from approved drafts
  human gate: voice choice (yours vs licensed AI voice) before recording
Step 2.6 — Brand kit

bv:brand
  outputs: brand identity doc, sales-page visual language,
           social-asset templates (Reels, carousels, Pinterest pins, YouTube thumbs)
Phase 3 — Optimization & Gates
Step 3.1 — QA pass

qa:verify
  checks: factual accuracy vs sources, internal consistency,
          accessibility (alt text, contrast), readability score,
          mobile-pdf rendering on iPhone
  output: pass/fail per chapter; blocking
Step 3.2 — Compliance pass

compliance:review
  checks:
    - every health/nutritional/safety claim has a cited source
    - no absolutes ("will," "cures," "guarantees")
    - lawyer-approved disclaimer on PDF cover, audio intro, sales page, checkout
    - refund policy meets FTC + Stan requirements
    - no impermissible IBCLC/medical-advice framing
  output: BLOCKING report
Step 3.3 — Human gates (cannot be skipped)

not done
Credentialed RN, CNM, or IBCLC reviews manuscript (paid $500–1500); redlines applied
not done
Lawyer approves disclaimer + refund policy ($300–500)
not done
You read every chapter end-to-end on an iPhone
Step 3.4 — Re-run after redlines

content:write  → apply reviewer redlines
qa:verify      → re-run
compliance:review → re-run

Step 6.4 — Strategy check against kill criteria

  cadence: every full sales cycle
  inputs: analytics + cohort behavior
  decision:

      → if 2 consecutive fails, invoke kill criteria
