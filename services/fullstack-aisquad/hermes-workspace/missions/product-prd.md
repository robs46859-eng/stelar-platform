# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## 4th Trimester Operating Manual

**Product**: 12-week postpartum digital guide (PDF + audio companion)  
**Target**: Prepared Paige — first-time mothers aged 29-36, $90K-$200K income, iPhone-first, seeks expert-vetted, time-saving solutions.  
**Objective**: Ship a high-value, scannable resource that reduces overwhelm, provides actionable guidance, and sells via Stan Store.  
**Success Metric**: 100 paid units before kill check; kill if <30 organic sales/cycle after 3 cycles.  

---

### 1. Problem Statement
First-time mothers experience intense anxiety, sleep deprivation, breastfeeding concerns, and overwhelm during the 4th trimester (weeks 0-12 postpartum). They turn to Google, Instagram, Reddit, and YouTube for answers but find fragmented, conflicting, or generic advice. They need a single, trustworthy source that:
- Is organized by week/theme for quick reference.
- Provides clear “is this normal?” guidance with red-flag tables.
- Includes partner-involved actions.
- Fits into micro-moments (e.g., 7-minute breastfeeding windows).
- Offers audio for hands-free consumption.

### 2. Target User: Prepared Paige
- **Demographics**: 29-36, bachelor’s+, dual-income, metro/suburban, on maternity leave or recently returned.
- **Psychographics**: High agency, high anxiety; time-poor; distrusts generic advice; trusts named experts and peer validation; will pay $30 to save 2 hours of Googling.
- **Jobs-to-be-Done**:
  - Get baby to sleep.
  - Fix breastfeeding pain/supply/latch.
  - Know what to eat for healing and supply.
  - Determine if feelings are normal or PPD.
  - Get body back without self-hatred.
  - Establish routines for 3-/6-month-old.
- **Watering Holes**: Instagram (Reels/carousels), Reddit (r/BabyBumps, r/beyondthebump, r/breastfeeding, r/sleeptrain), Quora, YouTube (pediatricians, mom vloggers).
- **Buying Behavior**:
  - Impulse window: 9pm–1am while feeding/rocking.
  - Price ladder: $19 impulse, $49 with one social proof, $99+ needs sales page.
  - Triggers: Specific scary moment (failed latch, can’t sleep, OB appointment).
  - Bundle-receptive: Hospital bag checklist + birth plan template + week-by-week beats single components.

### 3. Goals & Success Metrics
- **Primary**: Sell 100 units on Stan Store within 3 months of launch.
- **Secondary**:
  - Achieve ≥4.5/5 average rating from early buyers.
  - Generate ≥20 organic signals/month from engaged users (saves, comments, shares).
  - Maintain refund rate <5%.
- **Kill Criteria**: <30 organic sales/cycle after 3 cycles (≈90 days).

### 4. Solution Overview
A 12-week postpartum guide delivered as:
- **PDF**: Scannable, mobile-optimized, weekly chapters.
- **Audio Companion**: 90-minute MP3 for hands-free listening (while feeding, rocking, walking).
Each chapter follows a consistent structure:
1. **Body** (maternal recovery, nutrition, breastfeeding, supply)
2. **Baby** (sleep, feeding, development, soothing)
3. **Mental Health** (overwhelm, PPD screening, self-compassion)
4. **Partner Playbook** (actionable ways partners can help)
5. **Call Doctor?** (red-flag table: what’s normal vs. when to seek help)

Additional assets:
- **Registry List**: Expert-vetted newborn essentials (based on signal pull-quotes).
- **Decision Trees**: Interactive PDFs for top pain points (sleep, feeding, mood).
- **Audio Version**: Full manual narrated, chaptered for easy navigation.

### 6. Non-Goals
- Not a substitute for medical advice (clear disclaimer).
- Not a replacement for lactation consultant or therapy (encourages professional help when needed).
- Not a fitness or weight-loss program (focus on healing, not “bouncing back”).
- Not a long-form course (no modules, drip content, or homework).

### 7. Assumptions & Dependencies
- **Medical Accuracy**: Will be reviewed by RN/IBCLC and/or OB-GYN for safety.
- **Audio Production**: Can be recorded in-house or via freelancer; script derived from PDF.
- **Design**: Simple, clean layout; no need for complex illustrations (can use icons, tables).
- **Stan Store**: Platform supports PDF + audio bundle, coupon codes, affiliate links.
- **Ongoing Signal Flow**: Reddit and Quora monitors continue to feed signal-engagement swarm for optimization.
