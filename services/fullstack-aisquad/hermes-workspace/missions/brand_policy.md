# Brand Enforcement Policy: 4th Trimester Operating Manual

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Core Brand Attributes
- **Empathetic & Validating**: Acknowledges the emotional reality of postpartum experiences without judgment
- **Expert-Vetted & Evidence-Based**: Grounded in medical guidelines (ACOG, AAP, Cochrane, WHO) while remaining accessible
- **Time-Poor Friendly**: Designed for scannability and quick consumption in micro-moments (7-minute attention windows)
- **Practical & Actionable**: Provides concrete steps, not just theory
- **Inclusive of Partners**: Recognizes partner's role and provides actionable ways to help
- **Reassuring & Normalizing**: Addresses "Is this normal?" concerns with clear guidance
- **Signal-Informed Language**: Uses authentic language from maternal experiences (pull-quotes from Reddit, Quora, YouTube)

### Voice Characteristics
1. **Warm but Professional**: Like a knowledgeable friend who's also a healthcare expert
2. **Clear & Jargon-Free**: Medical terms explained in plain language when used
3. **Second-Person Perspective**: Directly addresses the reader as "you"
4. **Non-Directive Guidance**: Offers options and information rather than commands ("consider trying..." vs "you must...")
5. **Hopeful & Empowering**: Focuses on capabilities and solutions, not just problems
6. **Culturally Sensitive**: Avoids assumptions about family structure, feeding choices, or cultural practices

### Tone Guidelines by Section
- **Body/Maternal Recovery**: Informative, compassionate, focused on healing and self-care
- **Baby/Newborn Care**: Educational, reassuring, developmentally focused
- **Mental Health**: Validating, destigmatizing, supportive of help-seeking
- **Partner Playbook**: Practical, appreciative, focused on concrete actions
- **Call Doctor? Red-Flag Table**: Clear, calm, factual, anxiety-reducing through clarity

### Language Requirements
- **Must Use**: Signal pull-quotes for validation (exact phrases from research)
- **Must Avoid**: 
  - Judgmental language ("should", "must", "ought to" when describing maternal choices)
  - Fear-mongering or alarmist tone
  - Overly clinical or detached language
  - Generic platitudes without substance
  - Medical advice without proper disclaimers
- **Preferred Terms**:
  - "Chest/breastfeeding" (inclusive language)
  - "Postpartum" (not "after birth" when referring to the period)
  - "Newborn" (for baby 0-28 days)
  - "Caregiver" (inclusive of all parenting roles)
  - "Feeding" (neutral term covering breast, bottle, combo)
- **Avoid**: 
  - "Natural" (implies judgment)
  - "Breast is best" (judgmental and triggering for many)
  - "Just relax" (dismissive of real struggles)
  - "Sleep when baby sleeps" (unrealistic advice)

### Disclaimer Requirements
All content must include appropriate disclaimers:
- Medical disclaimer: "This guide provides general information and is not a substitute for professional medical advice..."
- Mental health disclaimer: "If you're experiencing symptoms of postpartum depression or anxiety, please seek professional support..."
- Feeding disclaimer: "Feeding choices are personal; this guide supports informed decision-making regardless of method..."

## Visual & Formatting Guidelines

### Document Structure
Each weekly chapter MUST follow this exact structure:
1. **Body** (maternal recovery, nutrition, breastfeeding/pumping, supply)
2. **Baby** (newborn care, feeding, development, soothing)
3. **Mental Health** (emotional well-being, overwhelm, PPD screening, self-compassion)
4. **Partner Playbook** (actionable ways partners can help)
5. **Call Doctor?** (red-flag table: what's normal vs. when to seek help)
6. **Citations/References** (evidence base for the week's guidance)

### Formatting Standards
- **Headers**: Use markdown hierarchy consistently (# for chapter title, ## for sections, ### for subsections if needed)
- **Blockquotes**: Use > for signal pull-quotes (exact verbatim from research)
- **Bold**: Use ** for key terms and emphasis (sparingly)
- **Tables**: Use markdown tables for red-flag comparisons and comparison guides
- **Lists**: Use - for unordered lists, 1. for ordered lists
- **Line Length**: Keep paragraphs short (2-4 sentences) for mobile readability
- **White Space**: Use clear section breaks and avoid dense blocks of text

### Citation Standards
- Reference specific guidelines: ACOG Committee Opinion #, AAP Policy Statement, etc.
- Include Cochrane Reviews and WHO guidelines when applicable
- Format: *[Organization] [Document Type]: [Title]* or *[Organization]: [Guideline Name]*
- Place citations at end of chapter in a dedicated section

### Signal Integration
- Each chapter MUST include at least 1-2 verbatim signal pull-quotes
- Quotes must be attributed to source (e.g., "— Signal from r/breastfeeding")
- Quotes should illustrate pain points, validation, or real experiences
- Do not alter quotes for grammar or clarity (use [sic] if needed for clarity)

## Product Name Usage
- **Official Name**: "4th Trimester Operating Manual" (note: "4th" not "Fourth", capital T and O)
- **Usage**: 
  - First reference: full name "4th Trimester Operating Manual"
  - Subsequent references: "the Manual" or "the guide" acceptable
  - Never abbreviate to "4TOM" or similar in consumer-facing materials
- **Trademark**: Consider ™ symbol on first use in formal documents

## Audio Companion Guidelines
- **Tone Matching**: Audio narration should match the written tone - warm, clear, paced for comprehension
- **Pacing**: Approximately 7-8 minutes per chapter when read aloud
- **Pronunciation**: Medical terms should be pronounced clearly but not overly clinically
- **Validation**: Audio should include the same signal pull-quotes as written version
- **Consistency**: Same voice talent throughout all chapters for brand consistency

## Prohibited Content
- Medical advice that contradicts ACOG/AAP guidelines without clear labeling as alternative perspective
- Endorsement of specific commercial products without disclosure
- Judgmental language about feeding choices, sleep training methods, or parenting styles
- Claims of curing or treating medical conditions
- Content that could trigger anxiety without providing actionable solutions
- Generic advice not grounded in evidence or signal research

## Enforcement Process
1. **Pre-Production Review**: Brand compliance check during content:write phase
2. **Medical/Legal Review**: Separate from brand review but coordinated
3. **Final QA**: Brand compliance verification before PDF/audio production
4. **Audio Production Check**: Verify tone matching and signal inclusion
5. **Launch Readiness**: Final brand compliance gate before Stan Store listing

## Deviation Classification
- **Level 1 (Minor)**: Inconsistent formatting, missing citation format, minor tone drift
- **Level 2 (Moderate)**: Missing signal quote, incorrect product name usage, unclear section heading
- **Level 3 (Major)**: Medical advice without disclaimer, judgmental language, missing required section
- **Level 4 (Critical)**: Contradicts evidence-based guidelines, contains harmful advice, missing disclaimer entirely

All Level 3 and 4 deviations must be corrected before publication. Level 1-2 should be addressed when possible but may be approved with justification.
